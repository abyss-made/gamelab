## Findings (code inspection, prioritized)

| # | Sev | Game | Finding | Status |
|---|-----|------|---------|--------|
| 1 | P0 | Word Chain | Validation used a ~2,000-word curated list plus the Draw & Guess bank; ordinary words, plurals and inflections ("walruses", "tortoises") were rejected as "not in the word list". | Fixed: any word in the bundled lexicon is accepted. | 6 | - | All other games | Full two-client browser audit of every game was not performed in this run. The existing suite (`npm test`, 30+ files covering sockets, bots, reliability, UI) was run and is reported in the PR. | Follow-up PR recommended. |

## What changed

- `server/data/lexicon.txt` (115k words, SCOWL sizes 10-70, five English dialects), generated by
  `scripts/build-lexicon.js`; attribution in `server/data/LEXICON.md` + `LEXICON-COPYRIGHT.txt`.
- `server/lexicon.js`: bundled, no network; normalization (case, whitespace, NFKD/full-width, accents, zero-width chars);
  `server/data/lexicon-extra.txt` is the exception list for omissions.
- Broad **answer validation** (lexicon) is now separate from **curated generation** (existing lists).
- Secrecy unchanged: views still never contain the scramble answer or other players' category answers
  (tested for Scramble).

## Spelling policy / limits

All dialect spellings accepted (color/colour). Proper nouns, abbreviations and hyphenated/apostrophe words are not in the
lexicon (add to `lexicon-extra.txt`). No list contains every English word; offensive words remain blocked by `server/filter.js`.
