# Property Tycoon · Playgrid Plaza

**Approved creative direction. Implement using [the shared handoff](../IMPLEMENTATION-HANDOFF.md).**

## Identity

Ivory map tiles, original neighborhood architecture and a muted rose property card; playful civic illustration.

## Composition

Revision: richer original miniature city with dimensional roof gardens, lit window details, canal, fountain, street furniture and tiny cars. Champagne tile edging, gem-like pawns and a Rose townhouse property card aim for the polish and collectible appeal of a premium property game. The underlying 36 tiles, names, purchase prices and rent remain Playgrid’s own. Alex is on tile 13, Blossom Avenue. Decorative rooftops and buildings in the central city do not imply purchased upgrades. Small house-shaped illustrations on compact tiles identify property type, not ownership or upgrade level. Four owned sample lots use separate lime dots.

Keep all 36 original tiles in place. A single contextual illustrated property panel replaces permanent management clutter. Phone keeps a visible, numbered tile map over a short detail sheet; full names and prices live in that sheet rather than unreadable micro-text.

## Interaction boundary

Use real Blossom Avenue: Rose district, $160 purchase, $12 base rent. Buy/Auction are existing choices. Other phases reveal rent/build/trade/end controls as needed; no copied Monopoly board or new economy.

## Motion story · intent, not final specification

Accepted roll → token follows existing tile sequence → property card opens → acquisition/payment/upgrade receipt. Ownership changes only on authoritative state.

## Required implementation states

Roll/travel, buy, auction, owned property, rent, upgrade/sale, trade offer/consent, debt/bankruptcy, match result, reconnect.

Approved: the original small-town art and contextual property card. Full group/building/transaction variants are implementation, not included final production art.
