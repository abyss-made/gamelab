# Reference analysis · Stage A

These are conceptual category references named in the assignment—not a claim to have run current competitor builds, measured their UX, or inspected licensed source assets. No competitor screenshots, logos, card faces, board textures, illustrations or sounds are included. The concepts use existing Playgrid identities and original review artwork.

| Game / reference | Adopt as a principle | Original Playgrid direction / deliberate difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Color Stack · Plato Ocho / modern shedding-card games | The hand is the main control; public counts and one clear discard pile anchor the social table. Selection must be inspectable. | **Neon Social Club:** plum felt arena, original angular card panels, restrained color glows, wide fanned hand. Do not copy UNO’s logo/oval trade dress or invent a final-card call rule. |
| Pool · Miniclip / Plato Pool | Let the table dominate; keep player identity, assignments and precise shot power at the perimeter. | **The Walnut Room:** warm wood and jewel-green cloth, quiet ivory markers, full portrait top-down alternative with upright ball numbers. No branded cue economy, spin wheel or unsupported bounce prediction. |
| Mini Golf · top-down arcade golf / Plato Mini Golf | Distinct ground materials, clean boundaries and a strong shot/result cadence create charm without visual complexity. | **Pocket Gardens:** botanical decorations outside the actual course, cream curbs and tactile green stripes. Repaint the existing Dogleg, then carry the approved language to Sand Trap and Water Bridge. Strictly 2D, no 3D or new moving hazards. |
| Ludo · Ludo King / Plato Ludo | Readable four-color ownership, a meaningful die, legal-token feedback and friendly perimeter avatars. | **Toybox Royale:** lacquered numbered discs, pale inset wells and ivory die. Clear token-list alternatives supplement tiny track squares; no copied app mascots, roll predictions or casino effects. |
| Property Tycoon · Monopoly GO! / digital property trading | Purchasing, ownership and upgrades need strong emotional and information hierarchy; show contextual decisions rather than every management panel. | **Playgrid Plaza:** original 36-tile map, original local architecture for Blossom Avenue, restrained property-card drawer. Keep existing Playgrid names/economy; no Monopoly board, tokens, named districts or 3D traversal. |
| Battleship · Plato Sea Battle / naval strategy | Separate private fleet from public targeting; deliberate coordinate selection makes dense boards usable. | **Bluewater Command:** two large nautical charts on desktop, one full-width targeting grid plus fleet tab on phone; original hull silhouettes, warm impact X and pale miss dots. No copied radar artwork or fake enemy information. |
| Go Fish · premium social card tables / illustrated Go Fish | A social seating arrangement, readable hand and memorable book collection are more important than ornament. | **The Marine Salon:** original engraved-fish card back, ivory deck and blue-green oval cloth, explicit Rank → Player → Ask rail. Rank/suit remain canonical; original marine motifs never change rules. |
| Chess · Chess.com / Lichess / premium digital sets | Prioritize the board, independent player clocks, unambiguous move layers and stable keyboard control. | **Walnut & Ivory:** warm 2D wood, cream/dark pieces using the existing original Playgrid silhouettes as a starting point, restrained move history. Board-theme preference is a proposed local feature; no copied proprietary set or fabricated analysis engine. |

## Common foundation, not one template

The slim Playgrid header, portrait sprite, party footer, typography, lime action treatment and focus ring are shared. Seating, board material, hand behavior, information density and control placement are game-specific. Chat/rules remain available from quiet perimeter controls rather than permanently shrinking every surface with the same right sidebar.
