ISSUE #58 / ORIGINAL LAYERED SVG CHARACTERS / DESIGN REVIEW · NOT IMPLEMENTED

One character family. Thirty-two new faces.

The home illustration is the reference.

Oversized chibi heads, dark expressive ink, layered hair/fur/metal, glossy eyes, navy clothing and lime/violet rim light. The original artwork and all 24 classic choices remain unchanged.

These new vectors translate that family into editable layers. Their finish is cleaner and less painterly than the raster reference; that remaining difference requires owner style review, rather than a claim of identical painted texture.
